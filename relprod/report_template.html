Which 4 products each PDP should recommend

How the 4 cards are chosen

  • Only linkable products. Published, not a test product, not excluded from search, and not a seasonal product out of season.
  • Funeral is a closed group. Any product tagged funeral, even alongside other occasions, links only to and from other funeral-tagged products. Grand-opening stands stay among stands.
  • No near-duplicates. Never the same product in another size, and at most one colour variant of the product being viewed.
  • Relevance blends shared occasion, recipient, gift type and flower (rare tags count more) with what shoppers did: sessions that viewed both, and sessions that viewed this product but bought the other.
  • Every product gets links. Each linkable product is recommended from at least 2 and at most 12 PDPs, so no page is orphaned and best-sellers don't take every slot.
  • Busy pages first. PDPs with the most traffic pick their conversion cards first. Coverage fixes go to quieter pages.

Rollout plan

    Before it goes live

    1. Review the busiest PDPs by hand. The first 50 rows (sorted by views) carry most of the traffic. Swap any card that a merchandiser knows is wrong; the rest can go in as generated.
    2. Import. product_related.sql fills the product_related table (product_id, related_product_id, prioritization 1 to 4) for every product in one transaction; product_related.csv has the same rows. Developers confirm that a lower prioritization shows first.
    3. Track clicks. Without a GA4 select_item event on related cards, their clicks are invisible. Fire select_item with item_list_name = related_products and the card position before launch.
    4. Test, don't switch. Configure half the products and keep rotation on the other half for 4 to 6 weeks. Compare PDP-to-purchase rate (any product), revenue per PDP session and related-card click rate.
    5. Regenerate monthly and before each peak (see seasons.py). Sets are fixed, so an unpublished card stays on the page until the next run.
